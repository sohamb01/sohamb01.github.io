Research

I am broadly interested in developing theory and statistically valid methods for problems motivated by applications, particularly in machine learning. My current research interest spans time series, learning theory, and stochastic optimization, with an emphasis on statistical inference under complex dependence. More recently, I have been exploring the theoretical foundations of generative models, including diffusion models.
